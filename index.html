The Team

Two generations, one line to the finish

HMT Racing Team is built around a simple idea: race together, learn together. Tom runs the 4500 class car, while his son Hunter cuts his teeth in Youth 1000 — both chasing checkered flags across Ultra4 and Best in the Desert's roughest terrain.

Before the desert, Tom served as a Green Beret — the same discipline, preparation, and calm under pressure that defined his service now shows up in how this team preps a vehicle and reads a race. That mindset is part of what he's passing down to Hunter, one race weekend at a time.
